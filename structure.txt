1. Features
Add, view, update, and delete bills.
Manage electricity, internet, mobile, rent, and other bills.
Record bill payments.
Track paid and unpaid bills.
View upcoming due dates.
Calculate total monthly expenses.
View payment history.
Save records using JSON files.
